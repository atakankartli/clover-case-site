Clover Case — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026

Clover Case is a puzzle game by Atakan Kartlı ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we collect: almost nothing

Clover Case has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own. Your game progress (solved levels, best times, settings) is stored only on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes that data.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect device information, including the device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / AAID on Android), IP address, and ad interaction data. This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses data in AdMob.

Tracking permission (iOS)

On iOS, the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any personalized advertising. If you decline, the app still works fully and ads are served non-personalized. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
